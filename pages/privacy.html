Privacy

I'm Ellis. I built NullPoint to catch scam emails, texts, and phone calls. I am not a carrier. I do not sell your data. You can delete your account anytime. The live policy is at /app/privacy on a deployed Signal Deck.

I collect your sign-in email, mailbox credentials you choose to connect (encrypted), message content needed to score threats, your grades/reports, and optional Call Directory numbers. Vendor lookups (IPQS, HIBP) only run when you ask.
