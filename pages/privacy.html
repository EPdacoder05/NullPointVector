Privacy

I built NullPoint to catch phishing, smishing, and vishing. I am not a carrier. I do not sell your data. Vendor lookups fail open. You can delete your account. The live policy lives at /app/privacy on a deployed Signal Deck.

What I collect: sign-in email, mailbox credentials you connect (encrypted), message content needed to score threats, grades and reports, optional phone numbers for Call Directory. Fleet intel is aggregates — not your inbox dumped to others.
