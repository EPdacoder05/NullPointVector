    Coverage
    
          
    Three channels, one campaign view.
    
          
    Each guard is tuned to how its channel actually gets abused — then the deck correlates them.
    
        
    
        
    
          
    
            
    PhishGuard
    
            
    Email
    
            
    Display-name spoofing, lookalike domains, credential-harvest pages, and attachment lures — scored against sender history rather than a static block list.
    
          
    
          
    
            
    SmishGuard
    
            
    SMS & RCS
    
            
    Short links hide their destination by design. Delivery-fee, toll, and bank-alert pretexts get resolved and rated before you decide whether to tap.
    
          
    
          
    
            
    VishGuard
    
            
    Voice
    
            
    Callback numbers, authority impersonation, and secrecy pressure are the tells in a voice script. Transcripts are read for coercion structure, not keywords alone.
