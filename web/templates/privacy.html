Privacy

Last updated: August 2026 · Pilot draft

Who I am

I'm Ellis, and I built NullPoint to help people catch scam emails, texts, and phone calls before they do damage. NullPoint is not a phone carrier, email provider, or bank. I do not place calls, read your mail without permission, or share your data with advertisers.

What I collect and why

Your account: email address and password (hashed — I can never see your actual password) so you can log in.

Mailbox credentials: if you connect Gmail, Yahoo, or Outlook, I store your app password or OAuth token, encrypted. I need it to read your incoming mail and check it for threats. I never read mail unless you've connected a mailbox.

Message content: subject lines, sender addresses, and body text from mail you've connected. I use this to score threats. I do not forward your emails anywhere. Raw message content may be deleted after 90 days.

Your grades and reports: when you mark something safe or block it, I remember that. It helps the system get smarter for you and for everyone else over time.

Phone numbers: if you add numbers to your Call Directory, I store those to sync your block/label list to your phone. I do not access your call history or listen to calls.

Billing: if you subscribe, Stripe handles your card. I never see your card number.

What I don't do

I don't sell your data. Ever.

I don't share your inbox with other users. Threat patterns (like "this scam campaign uses these numbers") are shared anonymously — not your emails.

I don't use your data to train models for other companies.

I don't track you across other websites.

Third-party lookups

When you run a phone or email lookup in the Identity tab, I send that identifier to a vendor (like IPQS or Have I Been Pwned) to check for known scams or data breaches. I only do this when you ask. Those vendors have their own privacy policies.

How long I keep your data

Raw email content: up to 90 days, then purged.

Your grades and block decisions: kept to improve detection.

Your account info: until you delete your account.

Billing records: as long as legally required.

Your rights

You can delete your account at any time from Account → Delete account. This removes your login, connected mailboxes, and personal data. Under GDPR and CCPA you can also ask to see, correct, or export what I hold.

Kids

NullPoint is not for children under 13. If you believe a child has signed up, contact me and I'll remove the account.

Changes

I'll update this page as the product grows. The date at the top shows when it last changed.
