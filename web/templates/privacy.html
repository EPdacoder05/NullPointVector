Privacy

What we collect, why, how long we keep it, and how you delete it.

Product. NullPoint (Signal Deck) helps you detect phishing, smishing, and vishing. It is not a carrier and does not place or intercept phone calls.

What we collect. Account identifiers you use to sign in; mailbox credentials you voluntarily connect (encrypted at rest); message metadata and content needed to score threats; grades and reports you submit; optional phone numbers you add to Call Directory lists.

Why. To score threats, improve the model via gated retraining, sync Call Directory / Message Filter lists, and process billing if you subscribe.

Retention (pilot). Raw message bodies may be purged after 90 days. Grades, report aggregates, and campaign fingerprints may be kept longer to protect other users. You can request full account deletion at any time.

Deletion. Use Account → Delete account, or email the operator. We wipe mailboxes, personal reports, and session data tied to your account subject.

Sharing. We do not sell personal data. Vendor reputation lookups (e.g. phone/breach signals) are consent-gated and fail open if the vendor is down.

Contact. Use Signal Deck → Connectors → request a provider, or your pilot operator contact.
