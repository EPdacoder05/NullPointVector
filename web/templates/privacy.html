Privacy

What I collect, why, how long I keep it, and how you delete it.

Who I am. I built NullPoint (Signal Deck) to catch phishing, smishing, and vishing. I am not a carrier. I do not place or intercept phone calls.

What I collect. The email you use to sign in; mailbox credentials you choose to connect (encrypted at rest); message metadata and content needed to score threats; grades and reports you submit; optional phone numbers you add for Call Directory.

Why. To score threats for you, learn from grades through a gated retrain (not a silent poison path), sync Call Directory / Message Filter lists, and process billing if you subscribe.

Sharing. I do not sell personal data. Vendor lookups (IPQS phone/breach, HIBP, Safe Browsing when keyed) are consent-gated where required and fail open if the vendor is down. Fleet intel is aggregates and campaign fingerprints — not your whole inbox dumped to other users.

Retention (pilot). I may purge raw message bodies after 90 days. Grades, report aggregates, and campaign fingerprints can stay longer so the next person is safer. You can delete your account anytime.

Deletion. Account → Delete account, or email me. That wipes mailboxes, personal reports, and your signup row. Env operator users cannot be deleted from process memory.

Kids. This pilot is not for children under 13.

Contact. Signal Deck → Account, or the operator who invited you.
