### Energy & Delivery:
- **Eye contact** - Look at camera, not screen
- **Smile** - Especially during Hook and Closer
- **Hand gestures** - Keep hands visible, use for emphasis
- **Pace** - Speak slowly (you'll naturally speed up when nervous)
- **Pauses** - Let key metrics land (pause after "98.5/100")

### Visual Transitions:
- **Face → Diagram** - "Let me show you how I built it..."
- **Diagram → Code** - "Here's the full stack..."
- **Code → Demo** - "Here it is running..."
- **Demo → Face** - "This isn't just a side project..."

### Common Mistakes to Avoid:
- ❌ Don't say "um", "uh", "like" (practice removes this)
- ❌ Don't apologize ("Sorry, this might be boring...")
- ❌ Don't undersell ("It's just a small project...")
